Karachi's Living Tradition

Why We Are Celebrated As "The King of Rusk"

For decades in Block M, North Nazimabad, early mornings have begun with the irresistible aroma of double-baked golden rusks rising from the ovens of New Madina Bakery.

Our secret lies in slow, unhurried baking, pure butter, premium flours, and roasted ingredients like peanuts and whole milk infusions. Every batch is baked to create that iconic hollow crunch that holds its crispness even when immersed in steaming Karak chai.
